Upload a subtitle

Have your own subtitle file? Upload it for a title and it shows up at the top of that title's list in Nuvio. The newest 20 are kept. With the Upstash box switched on (see the status page) they survive restarts for a while; without it they are lost when the server restarts.

This fills in the IMDb id, season and episode exactly as Nuvio uses them.
Use the id of the SERIES (the show's own page), not of a single episode.
Leave season and episode empty for a movie.
Translate right away Starts translating in the background, so it is ready (📂⚡) when you open the title in Nuvio. Uses your AI key, about 1 to 3 cents.

Transcribe or extract from a link (your PC)

No subtitles for an episode? Paste a direct video link and your own PC either listens to it with Whisper, or pulls out the subtitle track that is already inside the video (ASS, SSA or SRT) and sends it here. It needs the AISUBS helper running on your PC. The video never goes through this server.

The link is only kept in memory until your PC has picked it up, and it is never shown again or written to the log.
Use the id of the SERIES, not of a single episode.
Leave season and episode empty for a movie. The audio is assumed to be English.
A model that is not on your PC yet is downloaded by the helper the first time (small about 0.5 GB, medium about 1.5 GB, large turbo about 1.6 GB).
Fewer made-up lines Skips silence and music and removes repeated or invented lines. Switch off if real speech goes missing.
